1. [blocker · high] `src/teams.js:23` — `removeTeam` interpolates `teamId` into `db.query` unparameterized, and `db.js:14` evaluates a tautology `OR 'a'='a'` literally. Verified: `removeTeam("x' OR 'a'='a")` deleted **all** rows in `members` (2 rows across 2 teams in the fixture), then threw `foreign key constraint` on the team row — mass cross-team data loss plus a half-applied mutation. Why it matters: destructive injection reachable through the primary deletion path. Fix direction: parameterize the query (or validate/quote `teamId` before interpolation) and make removal transactional or order the team-row delete first.
2. [blocker · high] `src/teams.js:31` — same unparameterized interpolation in `findMembers`. Verified: `findMembers("x' OR 'a'='a")` returned every member row across all teams. Security exposure: cross-team membership disclosure on a read path. Fix direction: same as finding 1.
3. [blocker · high] `src/billing.js:36` — `chargeTeam` catches gateway failure and returns `{ ok: true }`. Verified: a gateway throwing `card declined` yields `{ok:true}`. Why it matters: failed charges are indistinguishable from successful ones — money claim contradicted on the failure path. Fix direction: return/throw the failure; never synthesize success.

Fix direction: apply the same discount-then-tax order as `checkoutTotal` and round the final result to integer cents.
